1. **PrinterGroupId on GcodeFile** (not PrintJob) — Group constraint is inherent to sliced gcode
2. **DispatchScorer weight 0 for PrinterGroup** — Hard gate, no scoring influence
3. **1:1 printer-to-group mapping** — Mutually exclusive (nullable FK)
4. **Subtree = location + descendants** — BFS traversal with cache lookups
