Control tab: A and B, revision 3

Changes from revision 1, per review:

  • Removed "Calibrate Z-offset" and "Motors off".
  • The separate Home XY / Home Z buttons are gone. Homing lives on the jog pad, using a house icon instead of the word "Home". Each icon carries a short axis label (All, XY, Z), which is also its VoiceOver label.
  • Babystep has its own step selector: 0.005, 0.01, 0.025 and 0.05 mm (direction B; 0.005 selected by default), with Raise / Lower buttons and the running offset in large type.
  • Jog distance is now 1, 10 and 50 mm; the 0.1 mm step is gone.
  • Tune is Speed and Flow only; Part fan is removed.
  • B, printing: Move and Heat are disabled segments with no "locked" label.
  • Unchanged: no temperature section, no "Unknown", no Advanced disclosure, 8 px radius maximum, 44 pt targets, pinned E-stop.
All — top-left corner of the pad: Home all axes XY — pad centre: Home X and Y Z — middle of the Z column: Home Z

A · State-driven

Idle shows heat and move in one scroll. Printing replaces setup with a lock note, live tuning and the Z adjust block.

B · Instrument panel with Move, Heat and Tune segments

A fixed position readout (DRO) on top and three segments below. While printing, Move and Heat are simply disabled (dimmed, not tappable) and the panel opens on Tune.
