Sensors & controls

Mainsail's direct controls, Fluidd's fan reset, PrintFarmer's styling. Enter or Tab sends a number. Release a slider to send its value.

Panel width

Fans: two compact lines

Name, optional RPM, reset and percentage across the top. A full-width slider underneath. Automatic fans have readings only.

Lights: a switch, not a form

A binary light uses an on/off toggle. It sends immediately. Brightness-capable outputs would use the percentage control instead.

Temperatures: aligned columns

Name, State, Current and Target. Sensors without a target have an empty cell. Missing readings are blank; a genuine zero remains visible.

No extra confirmation step

Number fields send on Enter or leaving the field, including Tab. Sliders send on completed adjustment, not every pointer movement. Reset sends 0% when the allowed range includes zero.

Still unmistakably PrintFarmer

Existing Mission Control colors, teal accents, tabular figures and compact corners. No separator lines between sensor rows.
