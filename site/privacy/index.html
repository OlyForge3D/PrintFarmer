PrintFarmer Mobile Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

PrintFarmer Mobile is a client app for PrintFarmer, an open-source, self-hosted 3D-printer farm management system. You run your own PrintFarmer server (or have one run for you), and the app connects to the server or servers you choose to add. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

In short: PrintFarmer Mobile does not include advertising or cross-app/cross-site tracking SDKs. The app talks directly to the self-hosted server(s) you add, keeps your sign-in credentials in the iOS Keychain, and keeps connection and preference data on your device. The only data that leaves your device toward OlyForge3D (rather than your own server) is the optional native push notification path described below, which you can turn off at any time.

Information we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, ad networks, or ad SDKs.
  • No cross-app or cross-site behavioral tracking.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or marketing SDKs are bundled with the app. The app's own "analytics" screens (for example print job history and maintenance insights) compute statistics from data already on your selected server and display them locally; they are not sent to OlyForge3D or any third party.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, on your device, so it can reconnect and remember your preferences between launches:

  • Server registrations — the address and display name of each PrintFarmer server you add, and which one is active.
  • Sign-in credentials and session tokens — stored in the iOS Keychain (not plain app storage), encrypted at rest, scoped to this device only (excluded from iCloud Keychain sync), and unavailable until the device has been unlocked at least once since restart. This does not require a passcode or Face ID/Touch ID prompt on every use of the app.
  • App preferences — settings such as theme, NFC tag format, advanced printer-controls opt-in, and push-notification opt-in.
  • Local caches — recent printer/job status snapshots and similar operational data fetched from your server, cached on-device to make the app feel responsive and to support brief offline viewing. These caches mirror data that already lives on your server; the app is not the source of truth for it.

None of this on-device data is shared with OlyForge3D. Clearing app data, signing out, or deleting the app removes it from your device.

How the app communicates with servers

PrintFarmer Mobile connects to the PrintFarmer server(s) you explicitly add — typically a server you or your organization operate on your own network or infrastructure. Public or remote servers (reachable over the internet, including by hostname or public IP address) are required to use HTTPS; the app will not connect to such a server over plain http://, so the address is rejected and you must use https://. Servers you add on your own local network — for example by IP address on your Wi-Fi, or a .local hostname discovered via Bonjour — may instead use plain HTTP, since that traffic does not leave your local network. In both cases, data you send and receive travels using whichever transport (HTTP or HTTPS) that server is configured for; the app does not add its own encryption on top of a server's chosen transport. The app may also use local-network discovery (Bonjour) to help you find a PrintFarmer server on your own Wi-Fi network. Your account data, printer data, job data, and files you manage through the app are controlled by that server and its operator, not by OlyForge3D. If you have questions about how a specific server retains or deletes your data, contact that server's operator.

Camera, barcode/QR, and NFC scanning

The app can use the device camera to scan barcodes and QR codes on filament spools and parts, and can read NFC tags on printers and spools, for quick inventory lookups. This processing happens on-device: the app decodes an identifier from the camera frame or NFC tag and does not capture, store, or upload photos, video, or raw NFC payloads. The decoded identifier (for example a spool or part ID) may then be sent to the server you have selected, the same way any other lookup in the app is sent, so that server can match it to inventory records it holds.

Printer camera (webcam) viewing

If a printer's webcam feed is available on your selected server, the app displays that video stream to you directly from your server. This is your printer's own camera feed, proxied by your server — the app does not access the device's camera for this feature, and does not record, store, or forward that video anywhere else.

Optional native push notifications

Push notifications are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, the app registers an Apple-issued device token with your selected PrintFarmer server. From there, delivery depends on how that server's operator has configured native push:

  • Disabled (default) — nothing is sent anywhere for native push; you still receive in-app and local notifications while the app is in use.
  • Relay mode — your server forwards a notification envelope to an OlyForge3D-hosted relay service, which forwards it to Apple's Push Notification service (APNs) so it can be delivered to your device. OlyForge3D operates this relay because the Apple Push credential for the app's App Store listing cannot be distributed to individual self-hosted servers.
  • Direct mode — an operator who has their own Apple Push credential can configure their server to talk to APNs directly, without the OlyForge3D relay.

The envelope forwarded for a push notification is limited to what is needed to show and route the alert: your device's push token, an app installation identifier, the notification's title/subtitle/body/category and thread, related printer/job/toolhead identifiers, a deep link back into the app, priority and expiration, available quick actions, and an identifier for the server the alert came from. It does not include your server credentials, account password, or files from your print jobs.

Data retention and logs

We describe retention in bounded, verifiable terms rather than promising guarantees we cannot make for infrastructure PrintFarmer does not operate:

  • Your selected PrintFarmer server stores device-push-token rows only while push is registered; a row is removed when you unregister, disable push, or your account is deleted, and is deactivated after repeated delivery failures reported by Apple.
  • The OlyForge3D-hosted relay (used only in relay mode) is an operational store-and-forward path to APNs. Like most push-delivery infrastructure, it may keep short-lived operational logs (for example, request/response status) for delivery troubleshooting, reliability, and abuse prevention. OlyForge3D does not use relay traffic for advertising and does not sell it.
  • We do not claim a specific guaranteed retention or deletion timeline for relay operational logs beyond what is stated here, because that infrastructure is operated centrally rather than per self-hosted install. If you need more detail for compliance purposes, contact us using the details below.
  • Data held on a self-hosted PrintFarmer server (accounts, jobs, printers, files) is controlled entirely by that server's operator and its own retention/deletion settings, not by this policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data leaves your device only to: (1) the PrintFarmer server(s) you explicitly add, as normal use of the app, and (2) when you opt in to native push in relay mode, the OlyForge3D relay and Apple's APNs, solely to deliver that notification.

Your choices and controls

  • Turn native push notifications on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Add, switch, or remove PrintFarmer server registrations in Settings; removing a server clears its stored credentials from the Keychain on your device.
  • Sign out at any time to clear your locally stored session for the active server.
  • Leave the camera/NFC scanning features unused — they are optional and only run when you open a scan screen.
  • Ask your PrintFarmer server operator to export or delete account data held on that server, since the app itself does not hold a separate copy of it.

Children's privacy

PrintFarmer Mobile is a tool for operating self-hosted 3D-printer infrastructure and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, call them out in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions, requests, or reports about this policy or the app's data practices can be filed as an issue in the project's public repository: github.com/OlyForge3D/PrintFarmer/issues. For questions about data held on a specific self-hosted server, contact that server's operator directly. PrintFarmer is open source; the source code is available at github.com/OlyForge3D/PrintFarmer.
